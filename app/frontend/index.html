How this works

A hybrid pipeline for oil and gas facility safety. A YOLO26 CNN finds fire and smoke in each frame. When a detection is sustained, NVIDIA DAM-3B describes the exact region, and an NVIDIA NIM reasoning model turns that into a structured safety advisory.

1. Live or upload. Start your camera, or upload an image or short video clip.

2. Detection. YOLO marks fire/smoke boxes in real time.

3. Advisory. On a sustained alert, you get severity, affected zone, recommended actions, and an escalation level.

Heads up: the first advisory after the app has been idle can take 30 to 60 seconds while the GPU vision model wakes up. After that it is fast.

Flare stacks, steam, and vapor can look like incidents; the model is told to weigh that and may flag a false alarm.
